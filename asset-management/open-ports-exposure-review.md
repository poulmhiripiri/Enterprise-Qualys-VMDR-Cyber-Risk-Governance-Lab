# Open Ports and Attack Surface Exposure Review

## Objective

Open-port analysis helps identify exposed services that may increase the attack surface of enterprise assets.

## GRC Importance

Open services should be reviewed regularly to confirm that they are required, approved and appropriately protected.

## Review Questions

- Is the service required for business or technical operations?
- Is the port expected on this asset type?
- Is the service exposed to trusted or untrusted networks?
- Is the service vulnerable or unsupported?
- Is there an owner for the service?
- Can the service be disabled, restricted or segmented?

## Governance Actions

- Review open ports by asset group
- Validate business justification
- Disable unnecessary services
- Restrict access using firewall rules
- Apply compensating controls where closure is not possible
- Track residual exposure in the risk register
