# Sample Cyber Risk Register

| Risk ID | Risk Area | Description | Impact | Likelihood | Rating | Owner | Treatment | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R001 | Vulnerability Management | Critical vulnerabilities detected on endpoint assets | High | Medium | High | Infrastructure Team | Patch and validate | Open |
| R002 | Patch Management | Missing critical patches on Windows asset | High | Medium | High | Endpoint Team | Deploy patch job | In Progress |
| R003 | Asset Management | Unknown or unmanaged assets may exist outside inventory | High | Medium | High | Security Team | Improve asset discovery | Open |
| R004 | Software Lifecycle | End-of-life software detected | Medium | High | High | Application Owner | Upgrade or remove software | Open |
| R005 | Certificate Management | Certificate expiry may cause service outage | High | Medium | High | Service Owner | Renew certificate | Open |
| R006 | Exposure Management | Open ports increase attack surface | Medium | Medium | Medium | Network / Security Team | Review and restrict unnecessary services | In Progress |
