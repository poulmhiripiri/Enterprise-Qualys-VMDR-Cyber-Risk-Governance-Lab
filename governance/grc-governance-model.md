# GRC Governance Model

## Purpose

This governance model ensures that cybersecurity asset data, vulnerabilities, patches and certificate risks are managed through a controlled, auditable and risk-based process.

## Governance Objectives

- Maintain accurate asset visibility
- Identify security weaknesses across the environment
- Prioritise remediation based on risk
- Track remediation ownership
- Improve patch compliance
- Reduce technology debt
- Monitor certificate lifecycle risk
- Provide audit-ready evidence
- Support executive decision-making

## Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| GRC Manager | Owns cyber risk governance, reporting, exceptions and compliance alignment |
| Security Engineer | Operates Qualys, validates findings and supports remediation |
| Infrastructure Team | Applies patches and resolves configuration issues |
| Application Owner | Owns application-level vulnerabilities and certificate renewals |
| Service Owner | Accepts or remediates risk for business services |
| Senior Management / CISO | Reviews risk posture and approves major exceptions |

## Risk Management Lifecycle

1. Asset discovery
2. Asset classification
3. Vulnerability detection
4. Risk scoring
5. Remediation planning
6. Patch deployment
7. Validation
8. Exception handling
9. Executive reporting
10. Continuous improvement

## Risk Acceptance Requirements

Where remediation cannot be completed immediately, the risk must be documented with:

- Asset name or asset group
- Vulnerability or issue
- Severity
- Business impact
- Reason for exception
- Compensating controls
- Risk owner
- Target remediation date
- Approval status

## Reporting Cadence

| Report | Frequency | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| Critical Vulnerability Report | Weekly | Security and Infrastructure Teams |
| Patch Compliance Report | Weekly / Monthly | IT Management |
| Technology Debt Report | Monthly | IT Leadership |
| Certificate Expiry Report | Monthly | Application and Service Owners |
| Executive Cyber Risk Dashboard | Monthly | CISO / Senior Leadership |
