# Training, Mentoring and Colleague Support Evidence

## Overview

In addition to the technical Qualys implementation, this project includes evidence of helping colleagues understand Qualys setup and implementation activities.

This strengthens the project because enterprise security roles require more than technical configuration. They also require communication, documentation, stakeholder support, knowledge transfer and governance awareness.

## Recruiter Value

This evidence supports the following professional skills:

- Qualys platform familiarity
- Vulnerability management knowledge
- Practical implementation support
- Peer mentoring and walkthrough delivery
- Security awareness and stakeholder communication
- Ability to document and explain enterprise security tooling

## GRC Manager Perspective

From a GRC perspective, training and knowledge transfer are important because cyber risk reduction depends on consistent operational understanding across teams. A well-configured platform only creates value when engineers, analysts and stakeholders understand how to interpret findings, prioritise risk and execute remediation.
