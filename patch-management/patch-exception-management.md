# Patch Exception Management

## Purpose

Patch exceptions are required when a patch cannot be applied within the agreed remediation SLA.

## Required Exception Details

- Asset name or asset group
- Missing patch or vulnerability
- Severity
- Reason patch cannot be applied
- Business impact
- Compensating controls
- Risk owner
- Target remediation date
- Approval status

## Example Compensating Controls

- Firewall restriction
- Network segmentation
- Endpoint protection monitoring
- Service hardening
- Temporary access restriction
- Increased logging and alerting

## GRC Rule

Exceptions should be time-bound, approved by the appropriate risk owner and reviewed regularly until closure.
