# Executive Summary

CloudNova Tech required improved visibility and governance across its hybrid technology estate. The Qualys VMDR and CyberSecurity Asset Management implementation was used to demonstrate how enterprise security teams can discover assets, identify vulnerabilities, prioritise risk, deploy patches, monitor certificates and report cyber exposure to management.

The project focused on four core areas:

1. Cybersecurity Asset Management
2. Vulnerability Management
3. Patch Management
4. Certificate Management

The implementation showed that effective vulnerability management depends on accurate asset visibility, risk-based prioritisation and measurable remediation. The lab also demonstrated the importance of software lifecycle visibility, technology debt reporting, patch compliance tracking and executive reporting.

## Strategic Value

This project provides evidence of enterprise cyber risk capability across:

- Asset visibility
- Vulnerability identification
- Risk-based prioritisation
- Patch remediation
- Certificate lifecycle governance
- Exception tracking
- Executive reporting
- Audit evidence preparation
- Continuous improvement

## GRC Conclusion

From a GRC manager's perspective, this exercise demonstrates the ability to convert technical telemetry into risk decisions, remediation governance and management reporting. This is the difference between operating a security tool and running a cyber risk management programme.
